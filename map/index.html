I could have sworn I used to know this place right here
I’ve forgotten my mother tongue
It’s happened to us both
We speak in a chorus of black box models growing larger
and consuming more
I don’t know how to understand what’s around
like a distorted lens has been placed over each eye

Won’t you plan a place for me
and plan a place for you too?

This is the complicated force they call a city
You have to see it to believe it
